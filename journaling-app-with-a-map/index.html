Why I built this

A journal you can wander

Remember is built by an indie studio from the Bavarian Alps. I kept a journal for years, but something always felt missing. The entries were there, but finding them — actually going back — meant scrolling through a list hoping to recognise a date. The memory was real. The app just couldn't show it to me.

Remember started from one idea: what if every entry could live where it happened? Not as metadata nobody looks at, but as the primary way you navigate your past. Open the map, see your life laid out by place, tap a pin and there it is.

It's a one-person project built for people who want to look back, not just write. If you have thoughts or questions, you can reach me directly at support@remember-journal.com.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about journaling apps with a map.

Is there a journaling app with a map?

Yes — Remember. Every entry can be pinned to an interactive Apple Maps view, so you can browse your life by location instead of scrolling a list. Tap a pin to see everything you captured at that place.

How is that different from a normal journal?

A normal journal is a chronological list. Remember adds place: you can filter by category, open the map, and rediscover entries by where they happened — not just when. It's a completely different way to look back at your life.

Is it free?

Free up to 100 entries — enough to genuinely see how the map feels with your own memories. A one-time €4.99 unlocks unlimited entries and all 100 categories. No subscription, no renewal.

Is my data private?

Yes. Entries stay on your iPhone — there's no account and no cloud. The app is made in Germany and is fully GDPR compliant. Nothing leaves your device.
