FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Remember – Life Journal?

Remember is an iPhone app that combines map, diary and photo album. You can capture experiences with location, date, photos and rating – and see them as pins on an interactive map.

Do I need an account?

No. Remember works completely without an account. All data stays on your iPhone.

Is Remember free?

Yes. The core features are permanently free. For €4.99 per month you unlock Plus – with advanced statistics, Home Screen widgets and PDF/CSV export.

Is my data uploaded to the cloud?

No. Remember stores everything locally on your device. There is no cloud requirement, no tracking and no ads.

Which devices does Remember support?

Remember runs on all iPhones with iOS 17 or newer.
