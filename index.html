The maker

Hi, I'm Florian K. — indie iOS developer from the Bavarian Alps. No corporate ties, no VC money, no growth-at-all-costs mentality. Just tools that respect you and your time.

Remember is a one-person project built on a simple belief: the software you use to remember your life should be something you own, not something you rent. I charge once, ship updates for free, and answer every support email personally.
